# Candy Boss 🎃

A tiny Halloween training game so your kids learn which candy to bring home.

1. **Parent setup**: tap each candy to rank it. One tap is ⭐ favorite (+10), a second tap is ❌ yuck (−5 and a lost life), a third clears it back to okay (+2). Rankings save in the browser.
2. **Catch the candy**: candy rains from the sky. Slide the pumpkin bucket left and right (finger, mouse, or arrow keys) to catch the favorites and dodge the yuck. Candy falls faster as time goes on.
3. **Game over** when the timer runs out (30, 60, or 90 seconds) or the bucket catches 3 yuck candies.
4. **Share screen**: score, star rating, a breakdown of catches and misses, a share button (uses the phone's share sheet when available, otherwise copies the text), the favorites to study, and a high score board.

## Play it

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step, no dependencies, works on a phone.
