# Candy Boss 🎃

A tiny Halloween training game. The parent ranks the candy they love (and the candy they can't stand). Then the kids go trick-or-treating: at each house they knock, a bowl appears, and they pick one candy. Picking the parent's favorites scores big, "okay" candy scores a little, and yuck candy loses points. By the end, the kids know exactly what to hunt for on Halloween night.

## Play it

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step, no dependencies, works on a phone.

## How it works

1. **Parent setup**: tap each candy once for ⭐ favorite (+10), again for ❌ yuck (−5), again to clear (okay, +2). Rankings are saved in the browser.
2. **Grab your bag**: the kid types their name and picks 6, 10, or 15 houses. The favorites are shown once more as a reminder.
3. **Trick or treat**: knock, pick a candy from the bowl, get instant feedback (and a hint if a favorite was sitting right there).
4. **Results**: a star rating, a breakdown of picks, how many favorites were missed, and a high score board for sibling rivalry.
